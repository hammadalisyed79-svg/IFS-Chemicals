# Distributor Portal — User Guide

## Login

1. Open **https://your-server/portal** (or main URL with distributor account).
2. Enter username and password provided by your supplier.
3. Change password when prompted.

## Dashboard

View outstanding balance, credit limit, and recent orders.

## Place an order

1. **Catalogue** — search products; enter quantity → **Add**.
2. **Cart** — review lines, add notes → **Submit order**.
3. Order status becomes **Submitted**, then **Under Review**.

You cannot edit the order after submission unless your supplier reopens it.

## Track orders

**My Orders** — filter by status:

Draft · Submitted · Under Review · Approved · Rejected · In Dispatch · Invoiced · Delivered · Cancelled

## Invoices & payments

- **Invoices** — download/view your sales invoices.
- **Payments** — upload payment proof (amount, date, bank reference).

## Notifications

Bell area shows order approved/rejected/dispatched and payment updates.

## Profile

View business details. Update password under Profile.

## Support

Contact your account manager for credit limit, pricing, or order changes.
