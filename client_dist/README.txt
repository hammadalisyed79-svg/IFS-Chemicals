IFS chemicals ERP — Client PC
=============================

OPEN THE APP
  Double-click  IFS_Chemicals_ERP.exe
  It opens your browser to https://erp.ifschemicals.com/

OR USE THE BROWSER DIRECTLY
  https://erp.ifschemicals.com/

LOGIN
  Use your username and password from the administrator.
  On first login you may be asked to change your password.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows PC with internet
  Chrome / Edge / Firefox

OPTIONAL
  Edit erp_url.txt next to the EXE if the ERP address changes.
